# Wireless Charging Pad

## Overview
Desk charger for devices that support Qi wireless charging.

## Features and connections
Qi-compatible wireless charger; USB-C input; power supply not included.

## Compatibility and setup
Compatible Qi-enabled receiver and suitable USB-C power adapter. Verify the receiving device supports Qi wireless charging and that the USB-C adapter can supply suitable power; a charger plug is not included.

## Care and maintenance
Place the compatible device centered on the pad and keep the pad dry and clear of metal objects.

## Shopping and support notes
This pad is intended for phones and accessories with Qi receiving capability. A USB-C input alone does not mean a phone supports wireless charging. Position the device centrally; thick cases and metal objects can interfere with charging. Customers must supply a suitable power adapter, because one is not included.

## Device and power compatibility
Look for Qi receiving capability in the phone or accessory. A USB-C charge port on a phone does not itself establish Qi support. The pad needs its own suitable USB-C power adapter, which is not included.

## Placement checks
Place the receiving coil close to the middle of the charging surface; move thick or metallic cases out of the path if the device does not start charging. Remove loose metal objects from the pad.

## Support example
If the charging indicator switches on but the phone does not charge, confirm Qi support, remove the case, reseat the phone and check the external power adapter before assuming a hardware fault.

## Additional product information
Charging power in watts, magnetic alignment, fast-charge classes and bundled wall adapter are not specified.

No. A compatible Qi-enabled receiver is required.

No. The stated specification excludes a power supply.
