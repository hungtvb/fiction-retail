# Interior Cleaning Cloths

Vehicle interior surfaces. Choose suitable cleaning methods for each vehicle surface; the cloths themselves do not include cleaning solution.

The pack contains six reusable microfiber cloths for vehicle interior maintenance. No detergent or cleaning chemical is included.
