# Wireless Earbuds

## Overview
Portable stereo listening and voice audio from a Bluetooth-capable source.

## Features and connections
Bluetooth 5.3; USB-C charging; stereo audio.

## Compatibility and setup
Bluetooth-capable device; USB-C power supply. Pair with a Bluetooth source; verify the source supports the desired audio and microphone behavior. USB-C is the charging connector, not a wired audio input.

## Care and maintenance
Keep charging contacts clean and dry; recharge through a suitable USB-C supply before diagnosing pairing failures.

## Shopping and support notes
Before choosing these earbuds for calls, confirm that the phone or computer can use Bluetooth audio and select a suitable microphone profile. When one bud fails to connect, charge both buds, check pairing availability, and reconnect from the host device. The charging cable is not an alternative audio connection.

## Pairing and audio profiles
First charge the earbuds and open Bluetooth discovery on the phone or computer. Select the earbuds as the output device; for calls, also check the microphone input selection. USB-C is used for charging, not as a direct wired audio connection.

## Fit and battery care
Keep charging contacts dry and clean; put the earbuds in the charging case between sessions. Battery endurance varies with playback volume and call use. A Bluetooth version number alone does not establish multipoint connectivity or noise cancellation.

## Support example
If music plays but a meeting app uses the laptop microphone, change the audio input device inside the meeting app. That is an audio-routing check, not necessarily a Bluetooth pairing failure.

## Additional product information
No rated battery runtime, water-resistance class, active noise cancellation, or multipoint pairing is documented.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can I connect these using only a USB-C cable?
No wired USB-C audio mode is specified; USB-C is documented for charging. Use a Bluetooth source for audio.

### Why do the earbuds not appear when searching for Bluetooth devices?
Check that the earbuds have charge and are available for pairing, then repeat Bluetooth discovery on the source device. The exact reset sequence is not documented.

### Are these suitable for swimming or rain?
No IP water-resistance rating is provided; water exposure cannot be recommended.
