# Portable Speaker

## Overview
Portable playback from a Bluetooth audio source for casual indoor listening.

## Features and connections
Bluetooth 5.2; 10-hour rated playback; USB-C charging.

## Compatibility and setup
Bluetooth audio sources. Pair with a Bluetooth-capable player; its quoted 10-hour playback figure is a listed rating, not a guarantee at every volume.

## Care and maintenance
Charge by USB-C; avoid covering openings and store away from moisture until a protection rating is established.

## Shopping and support notes
Use this speaker where Bluetooth playback is sufficient; the product description does not include a cable-input option. For use at a desk or in a room, consider where it can be charged without moisture exposure. The 10-hour figure is a rating, so do not plan an event around guaranteed playback at maximum volume.

## Audio source setup
