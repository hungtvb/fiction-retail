# Laptop Sleeve

## Overview
Padded protective carry sleeve for a laptop in the nominal 13-inch form-factor range.

## Features and connections
Polyester shell; padded compartment; 13-inch size.

## Compatibility and setup
13-inch laptop form factor. Measure the actual device body, including thick protective cases; the 13-inch label alone does not establish precise external fit.

## Care and maintenance
Wipe the polyester shell and let the sleeve dry completely before storing electronics.

## Shopping and support notes
The 13-inch label refers to a general laptop size class, not a universal fit. Customers should compare the laptop’s external width, depth and thickness, especially if a protective shell is fitted. A padded sleeve helps reduce routine scratches during transport but is not a substitute for a hard case in a drop.

## Fit verification
The 13-inch description is a laptop size class rather than a full internal-dimension guarantee. Measure the laptop’s width, depth and thickness, including any fitted protective shell, before selecting a sleeve.

## Protection level
The padded compartment helps with ordinary scratches and minor movement inside a bag. It is not a certified drop-protection enclosure. Keep liquids and sharp objects away from the sleeve.

## Support example
Two laptops with 13-inch screens can have different body footprints. A customer whose laptop has thick bezels should check exterior dimensions, not just screen size.

## Additional product information
Not a hard impact case and not documented as waterproof or certified for drops.

No moisture-proof protection is documented. Keep both sleeve and electronics dry.
