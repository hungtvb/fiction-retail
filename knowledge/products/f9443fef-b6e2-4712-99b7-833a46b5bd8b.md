# Ceramic Coffee Mug

## Overview
Reusable ceramic beverage mug with a nominal 350 mL capacity.

## Materials and specifications
Ceramic; 350 mL nominal capacity; hand wash recommended.

## Placement and everyday use
Not certified for microwave use. Use for ordinary drinks while leaving headroom; 350 mL is nominal, not a guarantee for every fill condition.

## Care and maintenance
Hand wash as recommended; inspect for chips and cracking before reuse.

## Shopping and support notes
The mug has a nominal capacity of 350 mL. Because microwave and dishwasher certification are not supplied, hand washing is the stated care option. Buyers should not interpret ceramic construction alone as confirmation that the mug is suitable for heating in a microwave.

## Additional product information
Microwave, oven and dishwasher ratings are absent; do not assume any of those uses are safe.

Microwave safety is not certified in the available specification.

The stated nominal capacity is 350 mL.
