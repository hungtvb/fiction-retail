# Travel Jacket

## Overview
Light outer layer with a nylon shell, zipper front and wind-resistant finish.

## Fabric and fit
Nylon shell; zip front; wind-resistant finish.

## Choosing the right fit
Outerwear, not waterproof certified. Suitable for general wind protection; select sizing based on outerwear measurements and planned layers.

## Care and maintenance
Follow the garment label for cleaning; do not assume high-temperature drying is safe for its finish.

## Shopping and support notes
Choose this jacket for an additional wind-resistant outer layer, not as a raincoat. If a customer needs dependable rain protection, look for a model with an explicit waterproof rating and construction details. Check outerwear sizing with any layers the customer plans to wear underneath.

## Additional product information
Wind resistance must not be confused with waterproof certification or a cold-weather temperature rating.
