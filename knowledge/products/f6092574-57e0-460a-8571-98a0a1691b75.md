# Desk Notebook

## Overview
A5-size paper notebook with 120 ruled pages.

## Materials and features
A5; 120 ruled pages; paper.

## Choosing the product
No digital synchronization. Use it for handwriting; no digital synchronization or app is included.

## Care and maintenance
Keep the pages dry and avoid bending the binding.

## Shopping and support notes
This A5 notebook contains 120 ruled pages. It is suitable for handwritten notes and does not connect to a phone or cloud application. Customers needing archival-quality paper, a removable refill or a particular paper weight should confirm those features separately.

## Additional product information
No paper-weight grade, refill format or archival certification is supplied.
