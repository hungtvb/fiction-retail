# LED Desk Lamp

## Overview
Compact indoor task lighting using a nominal 5 W LED and USB power.

## Materials and specifications
LED; 5 W nominal; USB power.

## Placement and everyday use
5 V USB power supply. Connect to a compatible 5 V USB power supply; the USB connector is for power, not data connectivity.

## Care and maintenance
Unplug before cleaning and avoid moisture near the connector.

## Shopping and support notes
This lamp requires a compatible 5 V USB power supply. A 5 W nominal specification describes electrical consumption, not a guaranteed brightness or desk illumination level. Customers needing dimming, color-temperature settings or a supplied wall plug should verify those features separately.

## Power and placement
The desk lamp is specified for 5 V USB power with a nominal consumption of 5 W. A computer USB port or external adapter must deliver compatible power. Position the lamp so it illuminates the work area without directing glare into the eyes.

## Choosing by task
A 5 W electrical input does not establish luminous flux, color temperature or dimming capability. Customers with specialized drafting or reading requirements should look for measured brightness and adjustability specifications.

## Support example
If the lamp does not turn on, first verify the USB supply and cable connection. Do not interpret a wattage rating as a statement of compatibility with mains electricity.

## Additional product information
Brightness in lumens, replaceability, battery backup, color temperature and dimming are unspecified.
