# Tire Pressure Gauge

## Overview
Battery-powered digital gauge for Schrader tire valves with 0–100 psi range.

## Fit and specifications
Digital; range 0–100 psi; battery powered.

## Vehicle and use compatibility
Valve type Schrader. Verify that the valve is Schrader and pressure is within range; confirm accuracy requirements separately.

## Care and maintenance
Keep the sensor clean and check the battery if the display does not turn on.

## Shopping and support notes
This digital gauge is designed for Schrader valves and reads pressures up to 100 psi. Verify the valve type on the vehicle or accessory before purchase. Accuracy tolerance and calibration certification are not given, so it should not be represented as a precision service instrument.

## Measurement procedure
Use the digital gauge on compatible Schrader valves; align the gauge squarely with the valve stem to minimize air leakage. Read and compare the pressure to the vehicle manufacturer’s specification, which is usually given on the vehicle placard.

## Range and limits
The instrument measures within its stated 0–100 psi range. That range does not establish a calibration certificate, accuracy tolerance or compatibility with other valve types.

## Support example
If a reading seems unusually low after disconnecting the gauge, check the tire for leakage and repeat the measurement with a secure seal. Never substitute a generic target pressure for the vehicle placard.

## Additional product information
No accuracy tolerance, other valve adaptor or calibration certification is supplied.
