# Wooden Building Blocks

## Overview
30-piece wooden block activity for children aged 3 years and older.

## Contents and age guidance
30 wooden blocks; ages 3+; small-parts warning.

## Age and use guidance
Adult supervision for young children. Check the stated age suitability and supervise play; younger children may be at risk from small parts.

## Care and maintenance
Wipe the blocks and inspect for damage that could produce sharp edges or loose pieces.

The set contains 30 wooden blocks for ages three and older. It is not an appropriate choice for a child below the stated age because of small-parts concerns. Adults should inspect pieces for damage and supervise play rather than assuming a safety test not listed for this set.
