# Car Phone Mount

## Overview
Adjustable-grip dashboard phone holder using an adhesive mounting base.

## Fit and specifications
Dashboard adhesive mount; adjustable grip.

## Vehicle and use compatibility
Verify surface suitability. Check dashboard surface suitability and the phone dimensions before attaching.

## Care and maintenance
Clean and dry the mounting area following adhesive instructions; avoid applying to delicate surfaces.

## Shopping and support notes
The adjustable dashboard mount needs a suitable clean mounting surface. Its adhesive fit is not guaranteed for every dashboard finish or texture. Before driving, confirm that the installed mount holds the device securely without obstructing visibility or vehicle controls.

## Additional product information
No specific phone-size range, heat-resistance rating or vehicle model list is given.
