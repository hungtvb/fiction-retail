# Tape Measure

## Overview
Five-meter steel measuring tape marked in metric and imperial units.

## Tool specifications
5 m steel tape; metric and imperial markings.

## Selecting and using the tool
General measurement. Choose the scale that matches the task and retract the blade with control.

## Care and maintenance
Wipe dry after use and avoid kinking the steel tape.

The five-meter steel tape carries both metric and imperial markings. Choose a consistent measurement scale for a project and retract the blade carefully. The listing describes general measuring use, not a certified calibration instrument with a published tolerance.
