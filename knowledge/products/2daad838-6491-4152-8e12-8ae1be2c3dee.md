# USB-C Hub

## Overview
Expand a supported USB-C computer with four USB ports and one HDMI output.

## Features and connections
4 USB ports; 1 HDMI port; 60 W passthrough.

## Compatibility and setup
USB-C host with video output for HDMI use. For HDMI use, the host USB-C port must itself support video output; having a USB-C connector alone is insufficient.

## Care and maintenance
Connect directly to a compatible host; use a suitable USB-C power source if supplying passthrough power.

## Shopping and support notes
When recommending this hub for an external monitor, ask whether the computer supports video over its USB-C port. A USB-C connector that carries data only cannot drive the HDMI output. Confirm the monitor input and cable before troubleshooting. The 60 W figure describes the hub’s passthrough rating; it is not a promise that every laptop will charge.

## Connection checklist
Connect the hub directly to the host USB-C port. For external display use, the host port must support video output, such as DisplayPort Alternate Mode; a data-only USB-C port will not send a picture. Start troubleshooting with a single monitor and a known-working HDMI cable, then confirm the correct monitor input. The four USB ports are expansion connections, not replacements for the computer’s host port.

## Charging and desk setup
The 60 W figure is the stated maximum power passthrough of the hub. It is not a guarantee that the connected laptop will receive 60 W after the hub’s own consumption or that every USB-C adapter is suitable. Confirm the power adapter and laptop requirements before promising a charging outcome.

## Support example
If a keyboard connected through the hub works but HDMI does not, the likely next check is the host’s video-output capability, rather than assuming the entire hub is faulty.

## Additional product information
The 60 W passthrough rating is not the same as guaranteed laptop charging. Supported HDMI resolution and USB data rates are not supplied.

## Frequently asked questions
### Why is there no HDMI image?
Confirm that the host USB-C port can send video, then verify the display input and cable. The hub cannot add video capability to a data-only port.

### Will 60 W passthrough charge every laptop?
No. It is a stated passthrough limit, not a compatibility or power-delivery guarantee for any specific laptop.

### How many USB peripheral ports are available?
Four USB ports are listed, plus one HDMI output; their individual speed classes are unspecified.
